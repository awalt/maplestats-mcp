### Added

- `nrcan_fuel_prices_list_locations` and `nrcan_fuel_prices_get_prices`: Natural Resources
  Canada's average retail fuel prices by city (issue #249). Regular, mid-grade and premium
  gasoline, diesel, auto propane and furnace oil, for 71 cities and Canada, in cents per
  litre with the taxes, marketing margin and refining margin in each price. Daily prices
  cover the last 43 business days; weekly (weeks ending Tuesday) and monthly averages go
  back to 2016. When NRCan serves a product at another frequency than asked (auto propane
  asked monthly comes back weekly), the result says so. NRCan's terms allow non-commercial
  reproduction only, and NRCan says the prices rely on third-party data; both are in
  `provenance`.
