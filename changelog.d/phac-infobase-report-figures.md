### Added

- `phac_infobase_*`: 23 more Health Infobase files, read live on 2026-11-19, in
  three new topics and one existing one: `perinatal` (seven figures from Trends in
  perinatal health during the COVID-19 pandemic: live births, antenatal and
  postpartum admissions, length of stay, c-sections, inductions, hypertension and
  diabetes, infant outcomes), `injury` (cold-related deaths, hospitalizations and
  emergency visits per 1,000,000), `antimicrobial_resistance` (nine CARSS figures:
  MRSA and VRE bloodstream infections, CPE, C. difficile, E. coli and K. pneumoniae
  urine isolates, gonorrhoea, drug-resistant TB, S. pneumoniae) and `health_status`
  (four AWCBC files on PTSD lost time claims). The catalogue now lists 78 files,
  all under the Open Government Licence - Canada.
