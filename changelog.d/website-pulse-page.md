### Added

- Website: a Pulse page (`pulse.html`, French `fr/pulse.html`), a running read
  on the Canadian economy. Thirteen headline indicators (real and monthly GDP,
  employment, unemployment, CPI and its trim and median measures, wages, the
  policy rate, bond yields and the 2-10 spread, the dollar, housing starts,
  commodity prices), four questions on inflation, jobs, growth and the bond
  market, seven charts, and StatCan's next releases. Every figure is recorded
  through the server's own tools (`scripts/capture_cases.py pulse`), and the
  Website workflow re-records it before each build and twice each weekday.
